**FL27 is fully released on Google Play.** After the beta and a round of further refinements, Football League 2027 version 0.2.22 is ready for everyone. Thank you to all the players who joined the beta and shared feedback along the way.

## What's new in FL27

- **Redesigned UI and control HUD**: a fresh look across the menus and a new on-pitch control layout.
- **More ways to play**: new quests, Local PvP and an enriched Career Mode.
- **Better football**: improved physics, AI, defending, shooting, goalkeeper reflexes, corner tactics and aiming.
- **Match-day atmosphere**: a new stadium, enhanced weather, and upgraded English, Spanish and Portuguese commentary.
- **New sound**: new soundtracks and improved sound effects.
- **Up-to-date squads**: updated player data and appearances.

## How to get FL27

- **Android**: update or download now on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.football.soccer.league).
- **iOS**: coming soon. Stay tuned.
