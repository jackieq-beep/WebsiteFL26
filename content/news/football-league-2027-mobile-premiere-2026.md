**Football League 2027 was revealed on October 28, 2026, at The Mobile Premiere 2026 in Bangkok, Thailand.** The new edition focuses on a deeper Career Mode, with competitive leagues, reworked training and transfers, and rewards at the end of every season. A release date has not been announced yet.

Football League 2027 shared the stage with some of mobile gaming's biggest names. NetEase showed major updates for Where Winds Meet and Sword of Justice, Darkest Dungeon II was confirmed for mobile, and Outfit7 Neo, part of the studio behind Talking Tom, revealed a new game.

## What's new in Football League 2027

The biggest changes are in Career Mode:

- **Competitive leagues**: a tougher league structure to climb through each season.
- **Updated training system**: new ways to develop your players over a campaign.
- **Reworked transfers**: an updated transfer system for building your squad.
- **End-of-season rewards**: prizes based on how your season finishes.

## Ways to play

- **Player Career**: follow a single player's journey.
- **Leagues and cups**: compete across domestic and cup competitions.
- **Daily challenges**: short goals to complete every day.
- **Local PvP**: play head-to-head matches with friends nearby.

## About The Mobile Premiere 2026

The Mobile Premiere is a new mobile gaming showcase run by GamingonPhone. Its first edition aired on October 28, 2026, just before the 6th GamingonPhone Awards. The show featured 22 reveals from 16 studios: 12 new games, 4 updates to existing games, and 6 titles from Indian indie developers.
